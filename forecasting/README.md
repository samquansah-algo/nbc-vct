**Which governs?** The v5 thesis and [ECONOMICS.md](ECONOMICS.md) govern. The four foresight studies were written in September 2026, before v5 narrowed the first customer. They are kept for their forecasts, signposts and benchmarks; their forecast numbering is separate from F1–F5, and where they disagree with v5 (for example on the beachhead), v5 wins. See [strategy/RECONCILIATION.md](../strategy/RECONCILIATION.md).

All projections come from stated assumptions. They are not forecasts of results and will be rebuilt from pilot data.
