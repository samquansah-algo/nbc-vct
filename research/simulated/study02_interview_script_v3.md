# Interview script (study 02): approved at checkpoint 2 (round 3)

> **SIMULATION STUDY.** This script is used for fictional rehearsal interviews and then revised into a field guide. Answers to it, real or simulated, are statements, not actions. Verbal interest is not payment.

**Rules for the interviewer**
- Ask about **concrete past episodes**. Default follow-ups: *"Walk me through the last time that happened."* · *"What happened next?"* · *"What did that cost, and who paid?"* · *"Who else was involved?"* · *"Is there a record of that?"*
- **Do not mention NBC, a node, tokens, AI or any product before C1.**
- Do not praise, agree or suggest answers. Accept "I don't know", disinterest and silence.
- **Role routing.** Questions marked **[Buyer]** go to people with purchasing authority (P01–P05); **[User]** goes to teachers and facilitators; **[Other]** goes to head teachers and parents. Where a question has variants, ask the one that fits the person's role. Always record the person's authority to *use*, *recommend*, *approve* and *purchase* separately.

## Warm-up (2)

**W1.** Walk me through a typical week in your role. What takes most of your time and energy?

**W2.** Tell me about the last time something new for teaching or learning was introduced where you work: a programme, some equipment or a service. What happened to it over the following months?

## Closing: the concept, the offer and willingness to pay (1)

*Read verbatim only now:*

> "Some providers are testing a shared learning set-up for schools and after-school centres. It is one solar-powered device with a camera, and sets of printed cards and pieces that children use in small groups to work through hands-on learning 'worlds', such as running a market or a water system. It works without internet. Teachers get guides and a short onboarding. Once a term there is a 'Show-Me Day' where children demonstrate what they can do to parents, and the school receives a one-page summary of what the class showed. The provider owns, repairs and replaces the equipment."

*Then read the offer (the approved offer from checkpoint 2):*

> "For a private school it costs $4 per participating child per term; for an after-school centre, $6 per child per term. [State the per-site total for this person's number of children.] The first term is a discounted pilot: $250 less than the normal per-site price for that term. You pay a 25% deposit of that first-term amount to reserve a place, refunded if the equipment is not installed on the agreed date, and the rest in two instalments during the term. Support includes a termly maintenance visit and help by phone or WhatsApp within one working day. You can stop at the end of any term with four weeks' notice and no penalty; the provider removes its equipment."

**C1.** **[Buyer]** Given your school's or centre's situation today, what would you do with this offer, and why? **[User/Other]** What would you do or recommend, and who would actually decide?

*Optional neutral probes:* Which budget would it come from, and when? · What would you need in writing? · What would have to be true for you to pay the deposit? · What would stop you? · How does this compare with what you spend on now?

*Record for buyers:* **Accept** (would pay the deposit on these terms now), **Negotiate** (would pay only if stated conditions or terms change), or **Reject**. *For non-buyers:* **Not assessable or outside buying authority**, plus what they would recommend and to whom.
