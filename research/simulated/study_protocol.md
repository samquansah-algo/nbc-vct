## 2. Segment boundaries

**Source-backed beachhead (S04 Step 2):** after-school and learning centres, plus fee-charging private basic schools, in the Central Region of Ghana, starting in Cape Coast.

**Founder correction (S10, DEV-014):** the research must "not be limited to Cape Coast or Ghana."

**Proposed boundary for the personas (to confirm at checkpoint 1).** The same two customer types, defined by what they do, not where they are:
- **fee-charging, low- to mid-fee private basic schools**, and **after-school or learning centres**;
- serving children of about 8–14;
- in low-resource urban and peri-urban settings with unreliable power and limited connectivity.

The proposed geography follows **a beachhead-anchored path from Ghana into the Global South** (founder question at checkpoint 1, round 1: *"Is this not a global challenge? Or is there any path from Ghana to the global south and the world?"*):
- **Ghana, the beachhead (5 personas):** Cape Coast and the Kasoa area.
- **Anglophone West Africa, the source-backed next step (2 personas):** Nigeria and Sierra Leone (S04 Step 14).
- **Further Global South path markets (3 personas):** Nairobi, Dhaka and an Indian city. The thesis names Nairobi and Dhaka as part of its long-term network (S01 §XXI). The spread of fee-charging private schools and after-school centres across these markets is **an external-market hypothesis, not yet researched in this study**.

Country-specific details in personas are fictional scenario assumptions. The study does **not** add adjacent segments (public schools, cafés, libraries, publishers, researchers) to manufacture diversity; every persona sits in the same two customer types. The purpose of the spread is to see which findings might **travel along the path**. One or two personas per country support no country-level inference.

> **Disciplined Entrepreneurship note.** DE asks for one beachhead. Spanning several countries is a research choice to test whether findings travel, **not** a change to the beachhead. If the founder instead wants a single-market sample, the personas can be regenerated for Ghana only.

## 5. Evidence rules for this study

1. Simulated answers can suggest hypotheses, sharpen questions and reveal missing definitions. They cannot establish payment, conversion, churn, setup time, unaided delivery, learning gains or transfer.
2. Willingness to pay is coded only against **one defined offer** (approved at checkpoint 2), and only for personas with purchasing authority.
3. No simulated quote may be used as a testimonial.
