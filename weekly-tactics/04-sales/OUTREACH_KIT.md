# Outreach kit: early customer demand generation

**Startup Tactics, Fall 2026 · Team NBC · Tactic week Wed 14 – Tue 20 Oct 2026**

## Rules
- Adults only, reached in their professional role. No children are contacted or targeted.
- One person at a time, from our own accounts. Never buy contact lists.
- Honour every opt-out at once. WhatsApp only after someone shares their number.
- Leads, sends and replies are real. Contact details stay in the private CRM, never in this public repo.

## Profiles and pass lines
| Profile | Who | Pass line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Program owner | Founder, director or coordinator of an after-school, holiday, club or STEM program (ages about 9–12) | 15% reply |
| 2 · School leader | Head, deputy head, ICT or STEM coordinator at a private, mission or international school | 10% reply |
| 3 · Sponsor | CSR manager, foundation program officer, partnerships lead with an education focus | 8% reply |

Overall: 10 first calls and 3 pilot offers with a stated price.

## Where to find leads
- **Program owners:** Code Club and micro:bit community maps; Africa Code Week and similar partner lists; makerspace and STEM-club directories; LinkedIn searches.
- **School leaders:** national private-school council member lists; mission and faith school networks; public district school lists; LinkedIn.
- **Sponsors:** CSR pages and annual reports of listed companies; foundation and grant directories; development-partner education programmes; LinkedIn.

Use [lead_list_template.csv](lead_list_template.csv). Record the source for every row.

## Messages
**1 · Program owner (email).** Subject: *Repeat your best session, without re-planning it*
> Hi [first name],
> I run Algo Peers in Cape Coast. For five years we have taught hands-on computing to 1,000+ children, and the hardest part was always the same: a great session depends on one great facilitator.
> We are now building Next Billion Children: ready-to-run Learning Worlds with materials, a facilitator guide and a shared AI helper, plus a short summary of what each child could do.
> Would you be open to a 20-minute call next week to see if a pilot fits [program name]?
> Sam Quansah · Founder, Algo Peers

**2 · School leader (LinkedIn).** *Your micro:bits, working every week*
> Hi [first name], many schools we work with have computing kits that sit unused because lessons take hours to prepare. NBC turns kits like micro:bits into ready-to-teach Learning Worlds, with a short record of what each pupil demonstrated, for parents and inspectors. We are opening a few pilot classrooms this term. Could I send you a one-page outline? Sam, Algo Peers

**3 · Sponsor (email).** Subject: *Hands-on learning you can report on*
> Dear [first name],
> Algo Peers has worked with 31 public schools and 11,000+ learners in Cape Coast. Most of their children never get to build and test real things.
> NBC delivers hands-on Learning Worlds in public schools, funded by sponsors, with a clear summary of what children demonstrated, and no children's data ever sold or shared.
> Could we share a short sponsorship brief and find 20 minutes to talk?
> Sam Quansah · Founder, Algo Peers

## Sequence
| Day | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Email or LinkedIn | The core message |
| 3 | Same channel | One-line nudge with a new detail (a 30-second Farm World clip) |
| 7 | Other channel | Connect on LinkedIn or email; offer the one-page brief |
| 14 | Email | Polite close: "Shall I close the loop?" |

A reply at any step stops the sequence. Send Tuesday to Thursday mornings, local time.

## Response types
Positive (call or brief) · Referral · Not now · No / opt-out. Log each in the CRM.
