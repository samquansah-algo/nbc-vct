# Milestones and go-to-market

*Plans and proposed pass lines, not results.*

## The next milestone

Show that **one NBC experience sequence**:
- can be delivered by facilitators outside our team;
- produces readable evidence of children's learning on an unfamiliar task;
- earns paid continuation at a support cost the buyer and NBC can sustain.

This first cycle tests feasibility. It prepares a stronger comparative study, and it does not prove broad effectiveness.

## Eight weeks

| Week | Work | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 programme owner and facilitator conversations; one learning problem and age band | Which learning decision and buyer to serve |
| 2 | 12–16 conversations in total; independent review of one capability and three task designs; a costed pilot offer | One offer and one bounded learning claim |
| 3 | Small task-usability sessions; a baseline check; offers to 10 qualified buyers | Fix the tasks; choose the least capture that is still useful |
| 4 | External facilitators deliver it; we record preparation, help given and children's explanations | Self-service kit, supported programme, or redesign |
| 5 | A delayed, unfamiliar task, scored blind | Is richer capture worth its burden? |
| 6 | Repeat delivery; record payments, buyer decisions and costs | Continue the paid pilot |
| 7 | Contrasting site if access is ready, otherwise a local repeat | Which parts travel and which need adapting |
| 8 | Review every gate | Repeat, simplify, change payer, or pause a part |

## Pass lines, set before we start

| Gate | Passes if |
|---|---|
| Paid demand | 3 of 10 qualified, independently approached buyers make a meaningful paid commitment |
| Delivery | At least two external facilitators each run repeated sessions within the quoted support |
| Learning | Baseline and delayed results, explanations and failure patterns can be read. Engagement alone is not a result |
| Measurement | Independent scorers meet an agreement standard set in advance, with uncertainty reported |
| Economics | Repeated delivery covers its direct costs, with full operating costs still shown |
| Another setting | Tasks for the same age group work well enough to read at a contrasting site |

If the data can't answer a question, we record "inconclusive" rather than moving the pass line.

## Go-to-market

| Route | Role now |
|---|---|
| Independent after-school and community programme owners | **First commercial test**, starting in Cape Coast |
| Families reached through Algo Peers | Discovery channel and a cheap test of a family offer |
| Schools | Eligible when the buyer, timetable and budget fit the same offer |
| Libraries, museums, makerspaces | Comparison sites, and distribution later |
| Governments and publishers | Scaling channels, once delivery and evidence are proven |

**Rules we keep:**
- Children are reached only through the adults responsible for them.
- We run one sales motion at a time. We will not launch sales to programme owners and a mass family subscription together.
- We add international sites for research before we sell internationally.
