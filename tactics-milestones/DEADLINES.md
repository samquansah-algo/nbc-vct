# Deadlines: Fall 2026

*Dated from the v5 Tactical Playbook (planning window 25 Sep–20 Nov 2026). Weeks run Monday to Sunday. A deadline is met only when the listed evidence exists; otherwise the item is recorded as missed or inconclusive, not moved quietly.*

## Eight-week plan

| Week | Dates | Work | Deadline / decision | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | 28 Sep–4 Oct | Send P1–P5; canvass Cape Coast programme owners in person; first 6 conversations | **4 Oct:** which learning problem and buyer to serve | 6 logged interviews; outreach screenshots |
| W2 | 5–11 Oct | 12–16 conversations in total; independent review of one capability and three tasks; costed pilot offer; send v2 messages | **11 Oct:** one offer and one bounded learning claim | Tracker; grid ratings; written offer |
| W3 | 12–18 Oct | Task usability sessions; baseline check; offers to 10 qualified buyers | **18 Oct:** fix the tasks; choose the least capture still useful | 10 offers sent and logged |
| W4 | 19–25 Oct | External facilitators deliver; log preparation, help given and explanations | **25 Oct:** self-service kit, supported programme or redesign | Facilitator logs |
| W5 | 26 Oct–1 Nov | Delayed, unfamiliar task, scored blind | **1 Nov:** is richer capture worth its burden? | Scores from two independent scorers |
| W6 | 2–8 Nov | Repeat delivery; record payments, buyer decisions and costs | **8 Nov: pass line, 3 of 10 buyers pay** | Receipts or signed commitments; cost sheet |
| W7 | 9–15 Nov | Contrasting site, if access is ready; otherwise a local repeat | **15 Nov:** what travels, what needs adapting | Adaptation log (F3) |
| W8 | 16–20 Nov | Review every gate against the pass lines set in advance | **20 Nov: closeout.** Repeat, simplify, change payer, or pause a part | Gate review in the decision record |
