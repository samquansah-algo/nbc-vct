# Deadlines: Fall 2026

*Dated from the v5 Tactical Playbook (planning window 25 Sep – 20 Nov 2026). **Weeks run Wednesday to Tuesday.** A deadline is met only when the listed evidence exists; otherwise it is recorded as missed or inconclusive, not moved quietly.*

| Week | Dates | Work | Deadline / decision | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | Wed 23 – Tue 29 Sep | Research, script and messages (done 23–27 Sep); send all 10 v1 messages; canvass Cape Coast (28–29 Sep) | **Tue 29 Sep:** v1 outreach complete | Screenshot of each sent message |
