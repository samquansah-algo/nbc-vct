# Problem interview script (PMR v1, NBC v5)

*For real conversations with adults only: programme operators, facilitators, librarians, parents and researchers. Children are never interviewed or contacted directly. Ready to use from 28 Sep 2026; revise after the first 6 conversations.*

## Before you start
- **Consent:** say it is voluntary, adults only, and that you take notes. Ask before recording; notes are the default.
- **Role check:** ask who uses the programme, who recommends things, who approves and who pays. Record these separately.
- **Balance:** at least half of operator interviews are with people not connected to Algo Peers, and both constrained and tech-rich settings are covered.

## Rules
- Ask about **real, recent examples**, not opinions about the future.
- **Don't mention NBC** or any product until question 9.
- Don't praise, agree or suggest answers. Accept "I don't know" and silence.
- Record refusals and the reasons given.
- **A compliment is not a buying signal.** Only money, time, a signed step or an introduction counts.

## Questions (about 20 minutes)

| # | Question | What it tests | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walk me through the last practical learning session you ran. | Context; the real routine | "What happened next?" |
| 2 | Tell me about the last time a child finished an activity but you weren't sure what they understood. | H1, P1 (can't tell if skill transfers) | "How often does that happen?" |
| 3 | What did you do next? What did that take? | Workaround and its cost | "Who else was involved?" |
| 4 | Show me how you record progress or explain it to a family. | P4 (hard to show families progress); H4 | "Can I see an example, with names hidden?" |
| 5 | Describe a time a learner used an idea successfully in a different activity. | What counts as transfer locally | "How did you know?" |
| 6 | What makes practical sessions hard to repeat with another facilitator? | H2, P2 (stops without the key facilitator); P3 (preparation time) | "Walk me through the last time the usual person was away." |
| 7 | What have you paid for to improve this programme? How was that decided? | H3; past spending; P5 (no budget route) | "What did it cost, and who signed?" |
| 8 | Which tools or routines work well enough that you'd keep them? | Existing alternatives | "What would make you drop them?" |
| 9 | *(Only after the problem)* Here is a scoped pilot: one Learning World, facilitator guide and short evidence summary. What would have to happen for it to fit? | Offer response | "What would stop it?" |
| 10 | What is the actual process for approving money, delivery and a start date? | Decision-making unit and sales cycle | "Who else should I speak to?" |

## Fields logged after each conversation
Role · setting (constrained or tech-rich) · connected or new · recent problem · workaround · consequence · frequency · buyer · budget process · existing spend · offer response · next concrete step · message version that led to the interview.

Then rate problems P1–P5 from 0 to 3 in the [insights grid](INTERVIEW_TRACKER.md#insights-grid): 0 means not raised, 3 means raised unprompted with a recent example.

## Hypotheses
- **H1:** Operators can't easily tell whether a child can use an idea in a new task.
- **H2:** Hands-on sessions stop when the one skilled facilitator is absent.
- **H3:** Operators would pay for reusable experiences and support that cut preparation.
- **H4:** Parents value seeing how their child tackles a challenge, not scores.
