# NBC go-to-market funnel model (synthetic)

Stages: contacted → attends a Show-Me Day → takes a discounted pilot term → pays term 2 → renews year 2. All rates and costs are assumptions.

| Channel | Contact → paid | Contacts per paying site | **COCA per paying site** | Year-2 renewal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Association talk (proprietors' network) | 7.2% | 14 | $875 | 75% |
| Referral from a paying school | 23.1% | 4 | $556 | 80% |
| Direct field visit | 6.7% | 15 | $1,360 | 70% |
| After-school parent funnel (centres) | 13.0% | 8 | $762 | 75% |

## Year-1 plan: new paying sites, contacts needed and acquisition spend

| Quarter | Cumulative paying sites (target) | New sites | Contacts needed | Acquisition spend | Blended COCA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 3 | 3 | 38 | $3,541 | $1,180 |
| Q2 | 6 | 3 | 37 | $3,105 | $1,035 |
| Q3 | 12 | 6 | 61 | $5,011 | $835 |
| Q4 | 20 | 8 | 67 | $5,671 | $709 |

**Year 1:** 20 paying sites, acquisition spend ≈ $17,328, blended COCA ≈ $866.

Reading: COCA falls as the mix shifts from direct visits to association talks and referrals. Referrals only exist once early sites renew, so Q1–Q2 are deliberately expensive. Compare with the business model's base-case COCA of ~$1,393 and LTV of ~$2,490 (private school) or ~$4,211 (school + evening cohort).
