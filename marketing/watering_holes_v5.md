# Where to reach NBC's users, buyers and research partners (v5 direction)

*Prepared 25 September 2026 for Sam Quansah (founder, Algo Peers; student at the Harvard Graduate School of Education). Desk research only. **Nothing was joined, posted or sent, and no one was contacted.** It supersedes an earlier v3 directory (Ghana private schools and the Node), which is not published. Ghana entries were carried over only where they fit the v5 target, and were re-checked.*

> **How far verification got (read first).** This environment's network policy blocked direct access to Facebook, LinkedIn, Reddit, Telegram, Discord and almost every association, forum and organiser site tried. Both direct requests and the page-fetch tool returned "egress blocked" (checked 25 Sep 2026). **Every entry below rests on search-engine indexes of the community's or organiser's own pages** (titles, snippets, cached counts). Nothing was opened while logged in. Four research passes ran in parallel, and the figures most likely to be wrong were spot-checked. One spot-check **contradicted** a research pass (the library makerspace group; see F1), which shows why each figure needs a live check before use.
>
> **No entry is rated High confidence.** Before any outreach, open each link while logged in and record the live member count, the last post date, the access type and the rules in the tracking sheet (§5.5).

## 1. Target summary (source-grounded)

**Sources read:**
- `strategy-v5/NBC_Thesis_and_Strategy_v5.md` and `NBC_Tactical_Playbook_v5.md` (founder-provided, 25 Sep 2026);
- `strategy-v5/RECONCILIATION.md`;

- the development log to DEV-021.

**Not accessible:** the NotebookLM notebook itself.

**The latest direction** (v5 §0, where the positioning is marked as the agreed direction; thresholds and sizes are proposals):
- the mission is children's learning and capability, with global scope;
- schools are one setting among several;
- Ghana is the development base;
- the first commercial segment is **programme owners of recurring hands-on learning programmes for children aged ~9–12**, starting with programme owners reachable in Cape Coast;
- a **contrasting research site** should serve comparable ages with substantially different access to AI or resources.

Adult and workplace markets are out of scope. Reconciliation decisions D1–D4 are still open, but none of them changes this recruitment target.

| **Who** | Facilitators, club coaches, librarians and museum educators running sessions, teachers in clubs | Programme owners and programme owners; school leaders where a school hosts the programme; parents (who pay programme owners' fees); sponsors | Learning scientists, assessment and measurement researchers, informal-learning researchers; practitioner-researchers | Ages ~9–12 in recurring programmes |

| **Existing tools and workarounds** | Unplugged activities, micro:bit, Scratch, Arduino and LEGO kits; teachers' own devices (v3 Step 3/11) [ASSUMPTION] | Showcase days, informal feedback to parents | Performance tasks, rubrics, observation | — |
| **Buying authority** | None; they use it or quietly don't | Full for programme owners (hypothesis); schools slower | None | None |
| **Information channels** (hypothesis) | WhatsApp, Facebook, practitioner networks (micro:bit, Scratch, Raspberry Pi) | Peer programme owners, associations, referrals | Societies, SIGs, conferences, mailing lists | Only through adult gatekeepers |

**Hypothesis segmentation added here** (to be tested, not assumed):
- **(a) Constrained settings:** will interviews show problems of *opportunity to practise, materials, facilitation and continuity*?
- **(b) Technology- and AI-rich settings:** will they show problems of *knowing what a child understands and can do when AI help changes*?

Neither group is presumed to have either problem.

**Children** are never contacted, identified or recruited directly. Any child's involvement goes through an adult gatekeeper (programme owner, parent or guardian), with consent and assent handled under the ethics gate (reconciliation A2).

  - follow up once;

| Role | Qualifies if | Exclude or label |
|---|---|---|

| Anyone | Adult, gives informed verbal consent to the conversation and to note-taking | **Minors: never** |

**Balance targets** (these are balance rules, not forecasts):
- at least half of programme owner interviews *not connected* to Algo Peers;
- both constrained and tech-rich settings represented before comparing them;
- a mix of people who have *and* haven't used physical-computing kits.

### 5.4 Revising messages from real responses
- **Few replies from a channel:** check that the moderator approved and that the timing was right before changing wording. Then shorten the request (voice note instead of a call).
- **Replies that praise but don't describe:** add "Can you describe the last time…" to the first line.
- **Replies that treat it as a sales pitch:** move "There's nothing to buy" earlier; remove any mention of products.
- **One problem dominating a segment:** make it the opening question for that segment. Never average across segments (v5 Workstream 1).
- **Keep a version log** so each reply can be traced to the wording that produced it.
