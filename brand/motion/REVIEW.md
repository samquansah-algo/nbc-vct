# Film review, 5 Oct 2026

The panel's lenses: brand strategist, ad and attention psychologist, sales lead, an Apple/Anthropic-style motion director, and a sound engineer and scientist. The verdict is followed by what was changed.

## Launch film: "Introducing Learning Worlds" (86 s), withdrawn 5 Oct 2026

| Lens | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | The first 0.5 s was blank and the first line arrived at 2.6 s; most viewers decide in 1–2 s | The piece appears at once, and "Answers have never been easier to get." lands in the first second |
| Motion and legibility | Captions were sized for a monitor, not a phone | All captions are 15–25% larger |
| Sales | It ended on emails, with no ask | The end card says "Partner with us on the Cape Coast pilot.", our first-stage goal |
| Brand | "Learning Worlds" is the hero; NBC is the maker; Algo Peers is the track record | "From Algo Peers" is added under the end card, for borrowed trust |
| Story | The order holds: tension (answers are cheap), insight (learning happens before the answer), product (introducing), proof (assistant, evidence), place (Cape Coast), scale (the next billion) | Kept |
| Sound | Natural, hypnotic palette; every cue moves with the new timings | The cue sheet was retimed |

## Teaser (9.5 s, 9:16)

| Lens | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | The first piece arrived at 0.4 s | It arrives at 0.12 s, and the build is tighter |
| Platform | "From Algo Peers" sat in the bottom reply-bar zone of WhatsApp and Instagram | Moved up into the safe zone |
| Psychology | "Something is taking shape." opens a curiosity gap without saying what we build | Kept |
| Confidentiality | Nothing in the teaser describes the product, the thesis or the mechanism | Confirmed |

## Recommended next

1. **Social cut-downs** of the launch film: 15 s and 30 s, vertical 9:16 and square 1:1, each ending on the partner call to action.
2. **Real footage** with consented adults (educators and facilitators) when the pilot starts: hands on materials, never children's faces without guardian consent and a clear purpose.
3. **A caption file (.srt)** for platforms that support it. Captions are already burned in for viewers watching on mute.
4. **A/B test the first line** in ads: "Answers have never been easier to get." against "AI can answer anything. Can a child build anything?"

# Red-team review of the brand-system films, 5 Oct 2026

The bar is an Apple or Anthropic launch film. The brand system was strong; these were the failures, all fixed in the current cuts:

| # | Failure | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The end card's hero moment read as noise: small, dim, scattered pieces | A bold row of five full-colour pieces snaps in, slides together and fuses into the white symbol, with five wood taps and a clay landing |
| 2 | Wipes grew from the top-left corner and sliced the previous headline | The piece now grows from the centre, as a deliberate brand gesture |
| 3 | Each new colour sat empty for about 0.3 s before its words arrived | Words start on the cut (+0.02 s) |
| 4 | The objects were too small to be heroes; the phone interface was unreadable | The ramp and car are 25–40% bigger; the phone is sized to the frame, and its interface type scales with it |
| 5 | "Shared AI." crowded the phone; "It holds." icons were tiny; evidence chips were small | Type gets its own column; icons are 30% bigger with "Ramp World" and "Plant World" labels; chips are larger |
| 6 | The master line and the Algo Peers endorsement were undersized | Both are enlarged |

**Still to come for the full standard:** real macro footage of the kits and educators' hands, with consent, once the pilot starts.
